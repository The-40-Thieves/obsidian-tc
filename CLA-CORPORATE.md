# The 40 Thieves Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

This is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review this Agreement before you rely on it.

This Agreement is for a company or other organization (the **"Corporation"**) whose employees or
contractors contribute to a project maintained by The 40 Thieves as part of their work. It grants
the Maintainer the rights it needs in Contributions the Corporation owns. Each person contributing
on the Corporation's behalf also accepts the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](./CLA.md).

## 1. Definitions

The terms **"Maintainer"**, **"Project"**, **"Submit"** and **"Work"** have the meanings given in
the Individual Contributor License Agreement, Version 1.0 (the **"Individual Agreement"**).

**"Designated Employees"** means the people listed in Schedule A, as the Corporation updates it
under Section 4.

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship that a Designated Employee Submits to a Project, at
any time before or after the Corporation accepts this Agreement, and in which the Corporation holds
any rights. Something marked clearly in writing as "Not a Contribution" when it is Submitted is not
a Contribution.

## 2. License grants

The Corporation grants the Maintainer, and everyone who receives a Work from the Maintainer, the
same copyright license, patent license and moral-rights waiver that Sections 2, 3 and 4 of the
Individual Agreement describe, for every Contribution, as if the Corporation were "You" in those
sections. This includes the Maintainer's right to license and sublicense the Contributions under
any terms it chooses, open-source or proprietary, and to change those terms at any time.

## 3. What the Corporation promises

The Corporation represents that:

1. It is legally able to enter into this Agreement, and the person signing has authority to bind
   it.
2. It owns, or has the right to license, the rights it grants in each Contribution.
3. Each Designated Employee is authorized to Submit Contributions on its behalf.
4. Each Contribution identifies any part that is not original to the Corporation or a Designated
   Employee, with its source and license.

## 4. Managing Designated Employees

The Corporation names its Designated Employees in Schedule A and keeps that list current by
notifying the Maintainer in writing (email is enough) when someone is added or removed. Removing a
person does not affect Contributions that person Submitted before the Maintainer received the
notice.

## 5. Other terms

Sections 6 (No obligation), 7 (No warranty), 8 (Limitation of liability), 9 (Assignment),
10 (Duration) and 11 (General) of the Individual Agreement apply to this Agreement, with
"the Corporation" in place of "You".

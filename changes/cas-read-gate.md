type: Security
---
- **A caller that may write a note but not read it gets only content-blind writes.** Creating, overwriting, `append_note`, `move_note` and `delete_note` still work. Anything whose outcome depends on the note's bytes is refused with one fixed `invalid_input` whatever they hold: any `prev_hash` it supplies, every `patch_note` operation (a frontmatter-preamble append or prepend included), `add_tag`, `remove_tag`, `update_frontmatter`, `update_task`, `bulk_set_property` (per note), the table, Kanban, canvas and base tools, `restore_note`, `prune_hub_links`, `copy_note` (which also needs `read:notes` on its source), the bookmark and workspace writes, `append_to_periodic_note` with a `heading`, and `update_excalidraw`. Under `writes.requireCas` such a caller cannot append to or patch an existing note. `rewrite_link` now needs `read:notes`, and it only scans and names notes the caller may read.
- **A write-only caller's `append_note` in `block` mode is judged on its own bytes.** A secret already in the note does not refuse the append, and the refusal names nothing in the note. `move_note` and `bulk_move` refuse a secret-shaped note in `block` mode with a refusal that names only the path.
- **The compare-and-swap detail is read-gated.** The `concurrent_modification` refusal, the `elicit_required` details and the write acks withhold `actual`, `prev_hash`, `quality_warning` and the whole-note `content_hash` unless the caller may read the note; `delete_note`'s `prev_hash` is `null` for such a caller, and `move_note`'s `content_hash` is `null`. An overwrite of an existing note always asks for confirmation for such a caller, empty or not. Readers see no change.
- **`patch_note` honours `writes.requireCas` and accepts `options.idempotency_key`**, like `append_note`. Under `requireCas` (on in the `hardened` posture), `patch_note` without `prev_hash` is now refused. `patch_active_file` inherits `requireCas` through `patch_note`, but it does not take `options.idempotency_key` (it strips `options`).

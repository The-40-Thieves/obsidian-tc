- **Contributors now sign a Contributor License Agreement.** Before a first pull request merges, contributors sign The 40 Thieves Contributor License Agreement (`CLA.md`; companies use `CLA-CORPORATE.md`) by posting the sentence the new `cla` check asks for. It gives the maintainer a permanent copyright and patent license to each contribution, including the right to license it under other terms later; contributors keep their copyright. The DCO sign-off on every commit still applies. Nothing changes for users: the software stays Apache-2.0.

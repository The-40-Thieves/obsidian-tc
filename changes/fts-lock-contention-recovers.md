- **A database lock at startup no longer turns full-text search off for the life of the process.** `ensureNotesFts` (and `ensureChunkFts`) used to swallow `SQLITE_BUSY` and remember `false`, so one contended moment left a server without its notes index and with no error or health signal (GH #1209). A lock is now a transient state: the server retries in the background after the first `initialize` with a bounded exponential backoff (at most 10 attempts, 1 s doubling to 30 s), switches full-text search back on by itself when the lock clears, and then runs one reconcile to backfill the rows written meanwhile. Searches use the exhaustive disk scan while it is off, so results stay complete, only slower. `server_health` and `get_index_status` gain an `fts` block (`available`, `recovering` with attempts and next retry, or `unavailable` with a reason: `lock_contention` once retries are spent, `disabled`, `no_notes_table`, `provision_failed`, `integrity_failed`; `last_error` only for an unrestricted administrator) and `fts_enabled` now follows it. `doctor --probe` reports a lock and a real failure (missing FTS5 module, unrepairable index) as distinct warnings instead of a silent "off".

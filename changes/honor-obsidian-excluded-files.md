- **Obsidian's Excluded files are honored by the index.** Entries in the vault's `.obsidian/app.json` `userIgnoreFilters` (Settings, Files & links, Excluded files), plus the new optional `vaults[].index.excludePaths`, keep a note out of the search index: no chunks, embeddings, full-text rows, contradiction or cluster detection, and it is never returned by `search_text`, `search_regex`, `search_semantic`, `search_jsonlogic` or `search_vault`. The pattern dialect follows the Obsidian 1.13.7 app: an entry is a case-insensitive path prefix, or a case-insensitive regular expression when written `/regex/`. As in Obsidian the note stays a link target: wikilinks to it resolve, and backlinks, outgoing links and `read_note` still work; the folder ACL is unchanged. Editing the list while the server runs reconciles the vault (newly excluded notes are de-indexed and their open contradictions dismissed; notes no longer excluded are indexed again), `doctor --probe` shows the effective list, and the eval golden-set guard skips excluded notes. `egress.excludePaths` is unrelated: it only withholds text from model providers.

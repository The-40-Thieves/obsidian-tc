- **Five denial-of-service advisories published against transitive dependencies are cleared: `minimatch` moves to 11.0.3 and `pyasn1` to 0.6.5.** `minimatch` 10.2.6 is vulnerable to denial of service via glob (GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249), and the fix is 11.0.1 — above the `<11` ceiling the root `overrides` entry deliberately held — so the floor becomes `>=11.0.1 <12` and resolves to 11.0.3. Crossing that major is safe here because the only package declaring `minimatch` is `@stryker-mutator/core`, a `packages/shared` devDependency behind the non-gating nightly mutation run; no shipped package depends on it, `brace-expansion` was already at the 5.0.12 that minimatch 11 requires, and v11 still ships a CommonJS entry. `pyasn1` 0.6.4 carries four quadratic-complexity denial-of-service advisories (GHSA-2qm8-x737-gmw5, GHSA-9m75-vc3g-4x39, GHSA-fm9c-5rcf-2q5p, GHSA-mm57-4v3p-rchx) and is relocked to 0.6.5 in `services/docs-ingest`. All five advisories were published after the last green scan, so this is a feed update rather than a regression, and no runtime behaviour changes.

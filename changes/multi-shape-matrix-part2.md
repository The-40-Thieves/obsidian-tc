- **The dark retrieval mechanisms and the shipped defaults are now measured on four corpus shapes, and every default carries a "validated on shapes" label.** `docs/EVALUATION.md` has a new section with per-corpus result tables (paired nDCG@10 delta with a bootstrap interval, permutation p, Benjamini-Hochberg result and a WIN, LOSS or TIE verdict against the preregistered minimum detectable effect) for evergreen, a code-documentation set, a Chinese personal garden and a generated multi-hop set; `docs/DARK_MECHANISMS.md` now records each mechanism's verdict and the shapes it was tested on. Nothing changes at run time and no default moves. Convex fusion loses on two of the three natural corpora and multi-query fan-out on all three, the default stack and the lexical stream hold up, and the three wins on the generated corpus are flagged as templated-query artifacts. The metadata prior and derived defaults changed no ranking and are reported as inert. The reranker re-test (needs a GPU) and the learned-sparse and ColBERT arms (need a multi-vector encoder) are pending, and LLM-inferred edges were skipped to avoid new spend. New tooling: `eval/corpora/matrix-report.ts` builds the per-corpus tables from a directory of run artifacts, and `eval/modal_rerank_gte.py` is the GPU scorer for the pending reranker arm.

- **`response_format` on knowledge reads, link-health tools, bundles and the canvas reader.** `vault_context`, `explain_answer`, `diagnose_retrieval`, `knowledge_get_critical`, `audit_provenance`, `vault_health_score`, `suggest_links`, `suggest_tags`, `bundle_files`, `bundle_folder` and `read_canvas` accept `response_format: concise|detailed` through the shared resolver, and `tools.defaults.responseFormat` now reaches the bundle tools too. Concise keeps what a caller acts on and every signal that qualifies it (the resume `cursor` and `missing_paths` of a bundle, `caveat` and `citation_pass` of an answer explanation, the prefetch age of a context bundle) and drops echoes, derivable counts and layout. Forty more tools (the Git, OCR, base, Excalidraw, Kanban, table, Dataview, MakeMD, Omnisearch and Remotely Save passthroughs and write acknowledgements, `reflect`, `knowledge_challenge`, the graph analysis tools and the canvas writers) were reviewed and are recorded as exempt with a reason in the user docs; the rest of the not-yet-covered list is left for a follow-up part. Unset and `detailed` output is unchanged; the advertised output schemas mark the concise-dropped fields optional. Part of #1027.

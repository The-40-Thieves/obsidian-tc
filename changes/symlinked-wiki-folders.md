- **A symlinked wiki folder no longer exposes `log.md`, and a symlinked raw folder is raw under its real name everywhere.** With `wiki -> pages`, `read_note("wiki/log.md")` and `read_note("pages/log.md")` now both need `read:provenance`: the implicit rule is installed on the wiki folder's real directory as well as its configured name (a wiki folder that cannot be placed inside the vault is refused at startup rather than left ungated; a vault whose root is missing at startup gets the rule on the real directory the first time its root exists, so a root that appears later with `wiki -> pages` is gated too, while a symlink made after startup in a vault that was present is picked up on the next restart). With `raw -> sources`, `sources/Topic.md` is now a raw note for duplicate detection (`find_existing_page`, `draft_wiki_page`, `commit_wiki_page`), `lint_wiki` and the scheduled lint, and the wiki judge, whose text is never sent: it was an "existing page" that blocked commits and reached the judge, contradicting "the judge never sees a raw note". Both placements are computed once per vault and shared. Provenance retention also now verifies every record it is about to delete (hash, columns against the signed body, link, signature) and refuses, logging the failing record, when one does not verify: a tampered `ts` column used to get the altered records deleted under a freshly signed anchor, leaving a valid, empty-looking chain. `find_orphans` and `audit_provenance` leave raw sources out too, and the generated `index.md` / `log.md` are recognised under the wiki folder's real name as well as its configured one (so neither a duplicate check nor the judge treats `pages/log.md` as a page).

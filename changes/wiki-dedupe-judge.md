- **Opt-in LLM judge for the wiki checks.** `find_existing_page` takes `judge` (default from the new `wikiJudge.enabled`, which ships `false`): an ambiguous verdict that rests on similarity or link text alone is resolved by the gateway `judge` model reading the topic and the opening text of up to 3 candidates (`same_topic`, `overlapping`, `different`); exactly one `same_topic` gives `exists` with `judged_by`, all `different` gives `new`, anything else or any failure stays `ambiguous`. Name, alias and wikidata matches are never overridden. `lint_wiki` takes `judge` and `max_judge_calls` to attach a verdict to near-duplicate proposals, and `maintenance.wikiLint.judge` lets the scheduled pass do the same. Bounded by `wikiJudge.maxCallsPerRequest`, `maxCallsPerDay`, `timeoutMs` (the deadline cancels the gateway request) and `maxNoteChars`; verdicts are cached per content hash and resolved model; notes outside the read ACL, `egress.excludePaths` or Obsidian's Excluded files are never sent. `obsidian-tc doctor` gains a `wiki.judge` check. A pre-registered study on the public corpus found the judge perfectly precise on topics but short of the recall lift needed to turn it on by default, so it stays off; results are in the Wiki checks docs. The gateway completion request gains an optional `signal` that cancels the in-flight request and its retries.

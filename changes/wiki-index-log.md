- **Generated `index.md` and `log.md` in the wiki folder.** A vault with `vaults[].wiki.folder` gets a server-built page index and a provenance projection whose paths are limited by the vault read ACL and Obsidian exclusions. `log.md` requires `read:provenance`; generated names are sanitised; both files stay inside the wiki folder, use snapshots, and are ignored as wiki content by dedupe, lint and link scans. A per-server HMAC seal bound to the vault id and file path keeps replaced files from forging authorship, while a matching legacy SHA seal triggers a one-time full rebuild that never trusts the old log body or cursor. Hand-edited and foreign files are left untouched and reported by `lint_wiki`. `commit_wiki_page` queues regeneration so large folders do not delay writes; its own provenance line appears on the next regeneration, and opt-in maintenance also rebuilds the pages on a schedule. See "Generated index and log pages" in the tools docs and "Server-generated wiki pages" in `SECURITY.md`.
